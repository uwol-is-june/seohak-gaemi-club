- 매출 "$200.6 billion, up 20% year-over-year"

- AWS 성장 "36.7% year-over-year, **accelerating for the fifth straight quarter**"
- AWS 규모 "now a $169 billion annualized revenue run rate"
- 칩 사업 "annual revenue run rate of **over $25 billion**, growing triple-digit percentages"
- AI 사업 "now also **over $25 billion**, growing triple-digit percentages"

- AWS 장기 "believe it'll be at least double that, and very possibly be a **trillion-dollar** annual revenue business"
- 백로그 "Our backlog stands at **$496 billion**, growing triple digits year-over-year"

- Q2 일회성 이익 "**approximately $1.2 billion**" — 관세 환급 ~$600M + 에너지 계약 회계이익 ~$600M
- AWS 영업이익률 "up **650 basis points** year-over-year. **520 basis points if you exclude the derivative accounting gain**"
- Q3 가이던스: 매출 "$197 billion and $202 billion"

## 4. ⚠️ 콜에서 다뤄지지 **않은** 것 (부재 자체가 신호)
