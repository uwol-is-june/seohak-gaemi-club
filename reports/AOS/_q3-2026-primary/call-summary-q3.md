# AOS Q3 2026 어닝콜 (2026-10-29) — 3자 요약본 (Motley Fool, 원문 아님 · 신뢰도 상한 🟡)

## Key Numbers
- Net sales $940.5M flat YoY; diluted EPS $0.93 vs $0.94
- North America $761.6M (+3%), segment margin 23.6% (-60bp). Boilers +13%, water treatment +5%, water heaters -1%
- Rest of World $192.8M (-7%); China -17% local currency
- 9M OCF $429M, FCF $396.2M; capex $33M vs $53M
- Buybacks YTD $260.6M (FY target $300M), dividends $149M; quarterly dividend +6%
- Cash $235M, net debt $439M, debt-to-cap 27% (from 9.2%, Leonard Valve)
- IEEPA tariff refunds ~$10M in Q3 (~$2M in Q2), none expected in Q4
- Restructuring $22.6M 9M — water treatment footprint (Groveport, Ohio plant closed)

## Guidance (ex-China)
- Sales growth ~4%; adj EPS $3.42–$3.52
- Boiler growth raised to 10–12% (from 6–8%)
- Water treatment cut to 3–4% (from 5–6%)
- Leonard Valve cut to ~$55M (from ~$70M)
- Steel and freight each up ~15% in 2026; NA COGS up ~5%
- Price increase up to 9.5% on most water heaters effective December
- NA margin held ~24% for year

## China divestiture
- CPE 81% at $400M EV; AOS keeps 19% + multi-decade brand license; royalty value ~$100M → total >$500M
- After-tax proceeds + repatriated cash ≈ $350M → buybacks after close (1H 2027)
- Shafer: "the market has changed significantly." / "the company that emerges from this transaction is a focused water platform anchored in North America."

## Analyst Q&A (요약)
1. Halloran (Baird): ex-China guide within July ranges; boilers offset softness; Q4 margin pressure from price-increase lag
2. Blair (Oppenheimer): price through inflation not offset internally; boiler growth = high-efficiency, pricing, share gains from competitor exits; normalized growth 4–6%
3. Summerville (D.A. Davidson): tariff refunds ~$2M Q2, ~$10M Q3; M&A focus core/water treatment/water mgmt; residential replacement "a little bit soft", proactive replacement >30%
4. Maklari (Goldman): water treatment backlog elevated from ramp at two facilities absorbing Groveport; dealer additions targeting independents
5. Jones (Stifel): $6M "other cost savings" structural (AOS operating system); channel inventory uncertain ahead of Dec price increase; lead times stable
6. Hammond (KeyBanc): royalty at arm's-length, rate undisclosed; competitors announced similar price increases (steel, freight)
7. Sano (JPMorgan): growth drivers boilers, commercial WH, regulation-driven upgrades, water treatment, water mgmt M&A
8. Graham (Seaport): steel pass-through industry-standard; new entrants face hurdle; buybacks ramp after close, $350M in 2027 planning
9. Kaplowitz (Citi): Leonard Valve heat-timer delays from policy changes, not structural; buybacks don't preclude M&A
10. Connors (Northcoast): some China cash needed to run business; $350M returned immediately upon close
